1. Choose the game's folder

Choose the folder that holds the game's files and its .exe program, for example the folder you installed or unzipped it into.

Use a computer for this step: phones and tablets cannot send folders.

2. Copy it to your PS5

Keep this page and MihawkOS open until the copy ends. If it stops, choose the same folder again: it carries on where it stopped.

PS5 home screen

Put a game on your PS5's home screen, as a tile of its own. The tile opens MihawkOS straight into that game.

Add a tile

3. The tile's picture

The game's picture with the MihawkOS mark. Give the game a picture on My games to change it.

Picture
How it fills the tile

Fill covers the square and crops the rest. Fit shows the whole picture. Stretch squeezes it into the square. Actual size shows it at its own size.

Drag the picture to choose the part the tile shows.

Your tiles

A tile you add or remove here reaches the home screen a few seconds after you close MihawkOS: tiles change only while no game or app runs, and MihawkOS is one.

Settings

The same settings as in MihawkOS's Settings on your PS5. A change is saved at once; games use it from the next time they start.

Playing

Controller

The controller your games think you are holding. Your DualSense works as any of them.

  • Xbox 360 controller: almost every game knows it. Choose it if you are not sure.
  • Xbox One controller: for games that ask for a newer Xbox controller.
  • DualSense: games that support it show PlayStation buttons. Games that only know Xbox controllers will not see it.

A game can have its own choice: open it on My games.

Game Mode

On: a game that fills the screen runs faster, and the Mihawk OS desktop pauses while you play. Off: every game stays inside the desktop. Turn it off only if a game's screen stays black.

120 Hz output

On: games can run at up to 120 frames a second on a TV that supports 120 Hz; choose 120 Hz in the game's own display settings too. Turn it off if your TV shows no picture or flickers when a game starts.

Theme and music

How the launcher looks and the music it plays. Your PS5 changes them as you choose.

Mouse

Mouse buttons

On the Mihawk OS desktop, the touchpad moves the pointer. Choose the controller buttons that click.

When something goes wrong

Detailed logs

Each game session keeps a detailed record, for a bug report. Leave it off unless you are reporting a problem: it writes more.

Help

Something is not working?

  1. Turn on Detailed logs in Settings, then start the game again until the problem happens.
  2. Come back here and download a bug report. Your network addresses are taken out of it.
  3. Attach the bug report to your message, and say what you did and what you saw.
